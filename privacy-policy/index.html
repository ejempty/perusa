Perusa

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Perusa is a comfort aid that shows visual motion cues over other apps while you ride in a vehicle. The short version: nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly turn on sharing, and your location never leaves it at all.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, rotation): read continuously while the cues run, used only to move the cues. Not recorded, not transmitted.
  • Location (GPS): used only to compute your speed, so cues appear only when you are actually riding in a vehicle. Speed is processed in memory. Your position is never stored and never transmitted — the app has no server-side knowledge of where you are or where you have been.
  • Foreground app package name (optional): if you grant Usage Access for the auto-pause feature, Perusa checks which app is in front so it can pause cues over apps you choose (password managers, banking). This check happens on-device; nothing is stored or sent.
  • Settings and drive history: your preferences, drive ratings, and tuning profile are stored in the app's private storage on your device only.

Data the app sends — only if you opt in

Sharing is off by default. If you turn on "Share anonymous drive data" in settings, Perusa uploads:

  • drive summaries (duration, distance, a motion-intensity score, your comfort rating, active tuning parameters, cue intensity),
  • crash and device-compatibility reports (Android version, device model, app version).

These are linked to a random install identifier generated on first run — not your name, email, phone number, advertising ID, or any hardware identifier. No location data (positions, routes, or coordinates) is ever included. The receiving server does not log IP addresses. Turning sharing off stops all uploads immediately.

If the optional road-test diagnostics logging is enabled together with sharing, detailed motion sensor logs (acceleration signals and speed over time — still no positions) are uploaded for debugging the cue engine.

What Perusa does not do

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to delete on a server.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no third-party data sharing of any kind.
  • No sale of data — there is nothing to sell.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device: uninstalling Perusa deletes it. If you opted into sharing and want previously uploaded anonymous records removed, contact the address below with your install identifier (shown in settings) — though note we cannot connect uploads to you as a person without it.
